## Important Legal & Operational Disclaimer

1. **Synthetic Dataset Disclosure:** All underlying member records, store locations, transactional logs, promotional offer responses, and experimental assignment vectors contained within this repository are **entirely synthetic**. They were generated via controlled stochastic simulation (`src/generate_synthetic_data.py`) to demonstrate advanced data engineering, cloud warehousing, and econometric modeling capabilities.
2. **No Proprietary Data Usage:** This project does not incorporate, expose, or derive from proprietary Starbucks Corporation operational databases, internal telemetry, or confidential financial records. 
3. **Portfolio & Demonstration Purpose:** Project Siren-LTV is developed strictly as a technical portfolio demonstration for hiring managers, executive stakeholders, and data analytics professionals. It does not establish official operational performance metrics, financial forecasts, or commercial strategies for Starbucks Coffee Company.
